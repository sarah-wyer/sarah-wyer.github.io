Framework

Knowing a model caused harm is not knowing who can fix it.

Discrimination DRIFT traces a discriminatory output back through the system that produced it, and identifies who is accountable at each stage. Most organisations can tell that a model produced something harmful. Very few can say at what point it entered the system, or whose job it is to fix.

Discrimination DRIFT: A Practical Framework for Responsible AI, published in AI and Ethics, 2026.

I left academia to work as a Data Architect at FDM Group, then returned to research and education. I know what these teams can realistically change.
