Framework

Discrimination DRIFT

Most organisations can tell that a model produced something harmful. Very few can say at what point it entered the system, or whose job it is to fix.

Discrimination DRIFT traces a discriminatory output back through the system that produced it, and identifies who is accountable at each stage. The point is practical: knowing a model caused harm is not the same as knowing which team can act on it.

Published in AI and Ethics, 2026.
